**Gmail triage** is a desktop application published by Emmanuel Bernard.
It helps you keep your Gmail inbox under control with the help of a large language model (LLM).

## What the App does

* Classifies and summarizes incoming emails so you can see what matters at a glance.
* Suggests and applies actions you approve: labels, archiving, draft replies.
* Uses your Google Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets for context and, when you ask it to, creates or updates events and documents.

You stay in control: you choose the permissions you grant, and you review the suggestions before relying on them.

## How it works

The App runs on your computer and connects to your Google account with the permissions you grant.
There is no server operated by me that receives or stores your Google data.
Relevant content is sent to the configured LLM provider only to perform the tasks you request.
