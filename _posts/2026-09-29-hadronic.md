A few months ago I **co-founded a company** in stealth, where I serve as **CTO**. It now has a name, a website, and a hiring page: [Hadronic](https://hadronic.com).

A few of us from the open source Java world got back together. We are building a platform to **develop and run AI-driven applications and agents** with the guarantees enterprises expect, **starting from the Java they already run** rather than from a blank page. Open source at the core. Sovereignty as a design goal, not a compliance checkbox.

![Hadronic banner](/images/blog/hadronic-blog-banner-1500x500.jpg)

Here is what I am after. I believe **Java has a strong future**. Not in a nostalgic way: it is tailored for the enterprise on more dimensions than any other stack I know, from the runtime to the tooling to the sheer number of people who can maintain and evolve a system ten years after it shipped. I also believe **AI and agentic systems are here to stay**, in one form or another, and that they will deeply transform businesses. Not in the big-bang fashion people tend to picture, but profoundly nevertheless. Put those two convictions together, and the conclusion writes itself. Enterprises will build AI-infused and agentic applications; **they will build them on Java**, and somebody has to make that a comprehensive, enterprise-ready experience. As the saying goes, the best way to predict that future is to invent it. That's what I intend to help drive as CTO of Hadronic.

I don't have much more to share yet. The website says a little more, and has a [newsletter](https://www.hadronic.com/#newsletter) if you want to hear when we step out of stealth. That is the best place to watch.

Our intention is to **start hiring soon** and if you're interested, please [reach out](https://www.hadronic.com/#hiring). If you've been involved in building **enterprise Java** (runtimes, frameworks), building **event-driven systems** or dived into AI-augmented enterprise apps, you're probably the type of person [we want to talk to](https://www.hadronic.com/#hiring).

This moment in our industry is extremely exciting. I can't wait to show how we want to shape it.
